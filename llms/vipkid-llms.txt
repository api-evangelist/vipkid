# VIPKid

> VIPKid (Beijing Dami Technology Co., Ltd. / 北京大米科技有限公司) is an online language-education
> company founded in 2013. It connects children with English teachers over one-to-one live
> video lessons and runs a global teacher marketplace, VIPTeacher. VIPKid publishes NO public
> API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable contract of any kind — this file is
> generated by API Evangelist from a third-party profile, not published by VIPKid.

## Agent-facing summary

- Public API: none. No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, gRPC/Protobuf or WSDL is
  published on any VIPKid host.
- Developer portal: none. developer.vipkid.com, docs.vipkid.com, developers.vipkid.com,
  openapi.vipkid.com.cn and developer.vipkid.com.cn do not resolve.
- MCP server: none. A2A agent card: none. Webhooks: none documented.
- Well-known documents: none. Every /.well-known/* path on vipkid.com, m.vipkid.com and
  vipkid.com.cn is 302'd to an Akamai domain-control-validation host; vipteacher.com 404s.
- SDKs: none. VIPKid's first-party open-source libraries are client-side courseware,
  HTTPDNS, bridge, logging and upload utilities — none of them calls a VIPKid API, and
  the most recent release is 2021-07-17.
- There is nothing here for an agent to integrate with. Treat VIPKid as a consumer service.

## Products

- VIPKid 1-on-1 online English lessons for children (mainland China and international).
- VIPKID AI 双师智学 — AI-assisted dual-teacher learning system.
- VIPKID 双语文化素养 / 大咖成人英语 / VIPKID 国际教育 — bilingual literacy, adult English,
  international-education programmes.
- Dino 亲子阅读馆 — franchised parent-and-child reading rooms.
- Lingo Bus — online Mandarin lessons for children (https://www.lingobus.com/).
- VIPTeacher — the teacher-side marketplace and portal (https://www.vipteacher.com).
